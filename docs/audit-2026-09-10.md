# aikit performance audit — 2026-09-10

**Tree:** `645295a` (2026-09-10, v1.39.1). Every `path:line` is keyed to that commit.

**Ask:** a whole-repo audit *mainly for performance* — where is aikit slower than it should be,
where does it do avoidable work, where does a documented fast path not engage on the shape
production uses, and where does a perf claim in the docs not match the code. Correctness stays in
scope (a fast kernel that is wrong is the worst perf finding there is) but is reported second.

**Scope:** all packages — `linalg/` (189 Go files + 25 `.s` kernels, both arches), `embed/`,
`encoder/`, `vision/`, `ann/`, `bm25/`, `hybrid/`, `topk/`, `sparse/`, `late/`, `chunk/`,
`fuse/`, `mmap/`, `bench/`, `benchmarks/`, `tools/`, and the `gpu/` module with its eight backend
sub-modules — ~95k lines of Go plus the CUDA and MSL sources; the perf docs (`cpu-acceleration.md`,
`perf-dead-ends.md`, `measuring-performance.md`, `roofline-2026-08.md`, `task-simd-audit.md`,
`task-native-gpu.md`, `BENCH-gpu*.md`) checked against the code; the 38 commits since v1.34.0.

**Method:** six independent reviewers (linalg kernels + dispatch; quant/formats/WeightMat/mmap;
encoder + vision; gpu; retrieval; harness + docs), each required to read `perf-dead-ends.md`,
`cpu-acceleration.md`, the roofline doc and the 2026-09-03 SIMD audit *before* the code, so no
recorded dead end is re-proposed and no known number is re-derived. Every finding cites lines the
reviewer read; every cost is bounded from the code's shape or from a number the repo itself
recorded (with its machine label). The consolidator re-read the source for every item in §1's
first two groups and §2, and checked the goinfer-side facts (which entry points goinfer actually
calls, at what M) against goinfer `c7ef16a`.

**Limitations:** static only — go1.24.7 against `go 1.27.0`, no proxy, no GPU, no arm64 host.
Nothing was built, run or benchmarked; every "×" below is either the repo's own measurement
(cited) or a count of instructions/bytes/launches from the code (marked *counted*). The audit
copy excluded goldens, tokenizers and model fixtures.

sits under `parallelThreshold` for L < 708 — ~⅓ of a lone L=512 forward on one core (M-05).
(4) P6a moved both ViT towers onto `AttendTileFused`'s f64 `math.Exp`, 2.7–3.1× per score element
over the f32 exp they ran before; at so400m the exp now costs more than the attention MACs
(M-09). (5) The activation quantiser runs serially over all M rows before every fan-out — 11–16%
of amd64 prefill in a loop no worker helps with (M-03). (6) The CUDA ViT towers run every int8

## §0 — Cross-cutting themes

1. **A win measured on one entry point, unreached from the other.** S-01b's tile went into
   `w8a8Span` and not `w8a8BatchSpan` (M-01); the row4 tile is behind `WeightMat.MatmulBTW4A8Into`
   and not the canonical span, the batch entry or the uniform `MatmulBTInto` (M-02, M-04);
   `FlatI8`'s batched GEMM exists and is reached only through the GPU shard split (M-18);
   `FlatI8.Query` inherits goinfer's decode `parThreshold` (M-19). The campaign's own rule
   ("test through its caller") applies to perf as much as to bits.
2. **Kernels shipped, callers not moved.** The five S-06 contract kernels (M-07), the NEON
   quantiser's amd64 half (M-03), the acc64 ports' amd64 half (M-06), S-02's dynamic chunking
   (M-08) — each was measured, recorded, and left one hop from production.
3. **Whatever stayed serial is now the token.** §7.15 made the encoder's linears 10× parallel;
   SiLU, layernorm and per-head attention did not move and are now a third of the forward
   (M-05, M-06). The same in `vision/`: the P6a head fan-out and the `Workspace` column split
   are the only parallelism; every activation, norm, bias and residual is one goroutine (M-10).
   And the quantiser before every fan-out (M-03).
4. **The wrong exp.** P6a re-introduced the f64 `math.Exp` and a separate f64 scale pass that
   item 13 had removed from the towers (M-09); `AttendTileFused` documents that as "part of the
   bits" for goinfer's raw-bit gate, which the towers do not use (their gates are cosine).
5. **The retired kernel that was only retired in one place.** `gemm_w8a8_tiled` measured at
   360 GMAC/s vs a 4876 roof and removed from `anncuda`; still every projection in `visioncuda`
   and `qwencuda` (M-12); `annmetal`'s batch GEMM is the same shape at ~3% of either M1 roof
   (M-16).
6. **The gate that cannot see it.** `perfgate`'s regex is two W8A8 M=1 benchmarks; the floor is
   ten samples re-derived per run; a blind row is green; a release can skip it with no record
   (G-01..G-04). Every finding above shipped under a PASS.
7. **Docs that describe the tree before the campaign.** `cpu-acceleration.md` still has the
   spin-park pool in four places (pulled, dead-ends §8.1), item 4's "compute-limited in both
   regimes" (single-core, contradicted by S-08.1), items 7–14 as landed wins (they are unreached
   kernels); `task-native-gpu.md` cites two deleted constants; `BENCH-gpu.md` promises a CI guard
   that does not exist; `perf-dead-ends.md` lists VNNI W4A8 as "no hardware" though it shipped.

## §1 — Major (performance)

### A. Quantized GEMM dispatch (`linalg/`)

#### M-01 · `MatmulBTW8A8Batch` bypasses the S-01b W8A8 tile — per-(row, column) `dotI8` at every M *(two reviewers, independently; goinfer-side reachability confirmed)*
- **Where:** `linalg/quant.go:486-507` (`w8a8BatchSpan`: its own column loop, `dotI8` per pair);
  contrast `linalg/quant.go:357-407` (`w8a8Span` hands the largest 4×4 arm64 / 4×1 amd64
  rectangle to `w8a8TileRect`) and `linalg/quant.go:806-818` (`w4a8BatchOp` routes through the
  span precisely to avoid this: "keeps amd64's M>=4 row tiling … working on the batched shape
  rather than being bypassed"). Callers: goinfer `decoder/forwardn.go:424,636` →
  `matmulW8A8Batch` → `linalg.MatmulBTW8A8Batch` at M = the prefill/verify row count.
- **Code:**
  ```go
  // quant.go:492-503
  for j := lo; j < hi; j++ {
      jj := j - base
      bj := op.BQ[jj*K : jj*K+K]
      bScale := op.Scales[jj]
      for i := range M {
          ...
          op.Dst[i*op.N+jj] = float32(dotI8(aq[i*K:i*K+K], bj)) * aScales[i] * bScale
  ```
- **Mechanism and bound:** an `int8int8` prefill row batches q‖k‖v and gate‖up — 30.6M of the
  44.4M MACs per 1.5B layer (69%) — through this entry; only down-proj (unbatched) gets the tile.
  Recorded tile gain on that shape: arm64 3.5–3.9×, amd64 1.17–1.44× (CHANGELOG v1.32.0). The
  W8A8 batch predates both tiles and was not revisited; `task-simd-audit.md`'s "every W8A8 caller
  at M≥4 gets it" is not true of this caller.
- **Fix:** `w8a8BatchSpan` calls `w8a8Span(aq, aScales, op.BQ, op.Scales, op.Dst, M, K, op.N,
  lo-base, hi-base)` per op, as `w4a8BatchOp`'s `canonical` closure does. Integer accumulation,
  so bit-identity is free; the existing `TestMatmulBTW8A8_MConsistent` is the gate.
- **Confidence:** confirmed (dispatch traced; goinfer's `forwardn.go` calls it at M>1).
- **Prior:** S-01b; new as a dispatch gap.

#### M-02 · arm64 has no tile on the canonical W4A8 span — paged MoE experts, never-repacked tensors, `MatmulBTW4A8Batch` at M>1 and the uniform `WeightMat.MatmulBTInto` all run the M=1 GEMV per pair *(two reviewers)*
- **Where:** `linalg/quant_w4a8_tile_other.go:13-15` (compiled on arm64: `w4a8TileRows` returns
  0), `linalg/quant.go:835` (`w4a8BatchOp`: `M != 1` → canonical even when `Row4` is supplied),
  `linalg/weightmat.go:215,238-239` (uniform entries route `q4` to the canonical free function,
  never the row4 method; no test pins it), `linalg/weightmat_row4_arm64.go:51-55` (paged experts
  are never repacked); the arm64 tile that exists: `linalg/matmul_w4a8_tile_arm64.go:29`
  (`MatmulBTW4A8Row4TileInto`, reached only via `WeightMat.MatmulBTW4A8Into` on a row4 tensor).
- **Mechanism and bound:** each output on those paths is `dotW4A8FoldSDOT` per pair at the
  recorded 24.1 GMAC/s against the tile's 69.5 (2.88×, `task-simd-audit.md` S-01) — the "int8int8
  beats int4 at prefill" mechanism. amd64 got a layout-free 4-row tile inside the canonical span
  (1.65–1.90×); arm64 did not. goinfer's dense int4 prefill takes `w.MatmulBTW4A8Into` on a row4
  tensor (`decoder/weightmat.go:415,494`) and *does* reach the tile; goinfer's W4A8 batch is
  M=1 only (`attention.go:87`, `mlp.go:441`). So on goinfer's side the reachable gap is paged MoE
  expert prefill and any tensor that declined the repack (K%32, N%4).
- **Fix:** an arm64 twin of `dotW4A8Tile4RowAVX2` on the canonical layout (one weight row
  unpacked once with the existing AND/USHR/ZIP1/ZIP2/SUB/SUB; four activation rows' SDOT pairs
  into four accumulators; the same per-output MOVI/SDOT/SDOT/SCVTF/FMLA order as `dotW4A8FoldSDOT`
  so bit-identity is by construction), hooked from `w4a8TileRows` on arm64; let `w4a8BatchOp`
  route M>1 row4 ops to `w4a8Row4TileSpan`; make `WeightMat.MatmulBTInto` consult the repack.
- **Confidence:** confirmed dispatch; reachability as stated.
- **Prior:** S-01 (its arm64 half was scoped to the row4 layout); new for the canonical span.

#### M-03 · The activation quantiser runs serially on the calling goroutine over all M rows before every fan-out — and is still scalar on amd64
- **Where:** `linalg/quant.go:673-675` (`MatmulBTW4A8Into`), `:790-792` (`MatmulBTW4A8Batch`),
  `:287-289` (`QuantizeActivationsInto` → `MatmulBTW8A8Into`), `:471-473` (`MatmulBTW8A8Batch`),
  `linalg/matmul_w4a8_tile_arm64.go:51-53`; `linalg/quant_act_other.go:9-14` (amd64:
  `maxAbsF32Scalar`, `quantizeRowScaledScalar`).
- **Code:**
  ```go
  // quant.go:673-675 — before the ws.parallel fan-out
  for i := range M {
      aScales[i] = quantizeRowInt8(a[i*K:i*K+K], aq[i*K:i*K+K])
  }
  ```
- **Mechanism and bound (counted):** at prefill M is 512+; the rows are quantised one after
  another on the caller while 6–8 cores idle. Per 1.5B layer 16,640 elements/token (12,032 with
  both batch forms) → 172–238M per K=512 prefill. Scalar cost ~2.4 ns/elem on the 3700X
  (`math.Round` alone 1.56–3.07 ns, dead-ends §6.3; the M1's scalar arm 3741 ns/1536, CHANGELOG
  v1.33.0) → **~0.4–0.6 s serial per prefill against the 3.6 s that box measured post-tile
  (141.7 tok/s, `task-simd-audit.md`) — 11–16% of amd64 prefill.** On arm64 the NEON pass
  (0.19 ns/elem) makes it ~1.3%. Per q/k/v call at M=512 the serial quantise (~1.9 ms) is
  comparable to the tiled matmul it precedes. S-06's stub measured only the transcendentals; this
  term has never been in a bound.
- **Fix:** (1) when M ≥ 4, row-split the quantise loop under the same fan-out before the column
  fan-out — rows are independent, bit-identical on both arches, divides the serial term by the
  worker count; (2) the S-03 amd64 half (AVX2 `VMULPS` + round-half-away).
- **Confidence:** confirmed mechanism; plausible bound.
- **Prior:** S-03 (amd64 half open); the serial-over-M structure is new.

#### M-04 · On AVX-512 VNNI hosts M>1 W4A8 has no tile at all — the S-01 mechanism is intact on the newest amd64 parts
- **Where:** `linalg/quant_w4a8_tile_amd64.go:33` (`if !hasAVX2 || hasAVX512VNNIVL || M < 4 …
  return 0`), `linalg/quant_w4a8_amd64.go:24-29` (VNNI preferred at M=1),
  `linalg/weightmat_splithalf_amd64.go:41-44`.
- **Mechanism and bound (counted):** the exclusion is correct for M-invariance (the VNNI fold
  uses two f32 accumulators, the AVX2 tile one), but a Zen 4 / Sapphire Rapids host then runs
  every M>1 W4A8 output as `dotW4A8FoldAVX512VNNI` per pair — ~23 instructions per 32 MACs per row
  (unpack ×M) against the AVX2 tile's 13 — ~1.7× slower per core on the prefill kernel than a
  Zen 2 host, in exchange for the M=1 kernel's 1.11–1.27× cold (`cpu-acceleration.md` item 4),
  which S-02 puts at ≤~5% of decode at 8 workers. Net-negative for any VNNI host that prefills;
  the docs record the exclusion as correctness and not its cost.
- **Fix:** (a) S-08.3's VNNI 4-row tile with the fold's two-accumulator order; or (b) immediate:
  on VNNI hosts route W4A8 at all M through the AVX2 fold+tile (keep VNNI for `dotI8`).
- **Confidence:** plausible (counted; no VNNI box in the fleet).
- **Prior:** S-08.3 "file, do not build"; CHANGELOG v1.32.0 "excluded by construction".

### B. Parallel shape and the serial remainder

#### M-05 · Text-encoder attention is single-core in a lone forward: the 12-head loop is serial and every per-head matmul sits below `parallelThreshold` for all L < 708
- **Where:** `encoder/attention.go:95-117`, `encoder/parallel.go:55-62` (`parallelThreshold =
  32_000_000`, with the comment "whose NET effect needs an end-to-end forward benchmark … a
  follow-up, not guessed at here"), `:72-80,147-158`; same structure in `forward_q8.go:171-187`
  and `attention_batch.go:73-98`.
- **Mechanism and bound:** `wantParallelMatmul` requires `M·K·N ≥ 32M`; with `headDim=64` that is
  `L² ≥ 500k`, L ≥ 708. CodeRankEmbed/BERT/rerank cap at `maxSeq=512`, so every per-head QKᵀ and
  scores·V runs serially and the head loop is serial; only `softmaxRowsScaled` inside it fans
  out. Per L=512 forward: 12 × 12 × 2 × 16.8M = **4.8 GMAC on one core** while the 58 GMAC of
  linears run on every core. At the repo's single-core blocked-kernel rates at these shapes
  (`linalg.go:25-27`) ≈ 350 ms serial against ≈ 0.6–0.7 s for the parallel linears — **roughly a
  third of lone-forward wall time on a 16-thread box**, growing as L². The campaign closed
  head-parallelism when QKᵀ was "~2.6% of Encode" (`cpu-acceleration.md:232-243`) — a profile

  `RepackInt4Row4`/`RepackInt4SplitHalf`; (3) **CORRECTED** (an earlier draft of this entry said

  (`isW4A8`) or `if q4, q4s, group, ok := w.Int4(); ok` (`matmul`/`matmulInto`), and `ok` is
  `false` for a repacked-only tensor by design (`Int4()`'s whole point is "canonical bytes
  present", not "is int4" — see the accessor rule above). Today, building any of these three
  sites' tensor repacked-only makes `isW4A8` return false, so `matmul`/`matmulInto` skip the
  int4 branch entirely and fall through to `f32, _ := w.F32(); be.MatmulBT(a, f32, ...)` with a

## §2 — Correctness

### C-01 · `qwencuda` uploads new segment bounds mid-forward on the null stream while earlier layers' `attention_seg` launches are still queued on the non-blocking stream — a write-after-read race that the tiny fixture cannot expose
- **Where:** `gpu/qwencuda/encoder.go:250-262` (uploads inside the layer loop when the block
  kind switches), `:280-284` (the launch that reads them), `gpu/cuda.go:347-364` (`upload`: copy
  first, `Synchronize` after), `gpu/cuda.go:328-334` (the stream model: "gocudrv creates every
  stream with CU_STREAM_NON_BLOCKING, which by definition does not order against the legacy null
  stream").
- **Failure:** the `Synchronize` added at `cuda.go:363` orders the copy before *later* launches
  (the RAW race it was written for); nothing orders it after *earlier* ones. At the first
  full-attention block (layer 7 in Qwen2.5-VL) the CPU issues `Upload(e.segS, FullStart)` while
  layers 0–6 are still queued; the null-stream DMA lands the full-image bounds under them, and
  their `attention_seg` reads `n = s1−s0` up to the whole image with shared memory sized to
  `MaxWinSeg` — an out-of-bounds shared write, garbage or a context-killing fault. On the pinned
  tiny fixture each layer's GPU work is shorter than its own enqueue so the queue never builds;
  the parity test passes (the same "hid behind small fixtures" pattern `cuda.go:337-340`
  describes). It also makes 14 full-device syncs per forward, against `encoder.go:26`'s "ONE
  Sync per forward".
- **Fix:** upload both bound pairs once per forward before the first launch and bind the right
  pair per layer. Independently, `Buffer.upload` should synchronize *before* the copy too (or
  issue on the queue's stream via `UploadAsync`) — `CopyDevice`/`CopyDeviceBatch`/`UploadBatch`
  carry the same earlier-launch hazard (`cuda_copy.go:61`, `cuda_upload_batch.go:84`).
- **Confidence:** confirmed by static trace on the stream semantics the file documents; not run.

### C-02 · `qwenmetal` full-attention blocks silently produce garbage above 8192 patches — threadgroup memory over budget aborts the command buffer and `Run1DTG` never reads the status
- **Where:** `gpu/qwenmetal/encoder.go:302-303` (`maxSeg*4` threadgroup bytes, no check vs
  `MaxThreadgroupMemoryLength`), `gpu/metal.go:901-921` (`Run1DBatchTG`: commit, wait, return —
  no `captureErr`, unlike `Encoder.End`/`WaitDone` at `:711-728`), `gpu/metal.go:157-160` (the
  documented obligation: "callers that size threadgroup scratch from model dims must check
  against this and decline (goinfer audit M-11)").
- **Failure:** Apple GPUs report ~32 KiB; `maxSeg*4` exceeds it at n > 8192 patches (≈1.6 MP at
  14-px; Qwen2.5-VL's HF default `max_pixels` is 12.8 MP). The abort is invisible; `e.att` keeps
  the previous layer's contents and the forward returns a plausible, wrong hidden state. CUDA's
  analogue fails the launch with an error.
- **Fix:** compare `MaxFullSeg*4 + 2*ViTBlock*4` with `MaxThreadgroupMemoryLength()` in `ensure`
  and decline; add `captureErr` to `Run1DBatchTG`.
- **Confidence:** confirmed mechanism; reachability depends on the caller's smart-resize cap.

### C-03 · bm25 WAND's upper bound is unsound for `B > 1` — `wandUsable` admits it, and the bound's monotonicity fails once the length-normalisation term goes negative
- **Where:** `bm25/wand.go:123` (`return ix.K1 >= 0 && ix.B >= 0`), `:165` (`ub := idf·(tf·k1p1) /
  (tf + k1·(1−B+B·minNorm))`), `:244` (the real score with `norm`).
- **Failure:** for B > 1 the denominator `tf + k1·(1−B+B·norm)` can cross zero, so the "upper"
  bound computed at `minNorm` is not an upper bound; numerically checked by the reviewer: bound
  4.17 vs a real posting scoring 25 — `TopK` silently drops docs the exhaustive path returns.
- **Fix:** `wandUsable` requires `B <= 1` (the standard range) — one condition.
- **Confidence:** confirmed (arithmetic).

### C-04 · `annmetal` has no OOM recovery: a corpus or batch that exceeds memory panics `EnableGPU`/`QueryBatch` instead of falling through
- **Where:** `gpu/annmetal/backend.go:298-316,377-381,422-429` (no `recover` around
  `MustBuf`-backed constructors); contrast `gpu/anncuda/backend.go:135-139,232-236,590-594`;

### C-05 · `Workspace.QuantizeActivations` documents a scratch lifetime that its own `MatmulBTQ8Into`/`MatmulBTQ4Into` violate *(two reviewers)*
- **Where:** `linalg/workspace.go:83-88` ("valid until the next call that grows the Workspace's

### C-06 · `vision.Preprocess` with `Config.MaxPixels == 0` silently disables the decompression-bomb guard; Gemma 4 `averagePool` divides every bucket by k² without checking occupancy
